Questions

Good to know

Is the instant estimate really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation. You'll see your price options whether or not you decide to move forward.

How accurate is it?

It uses satellite measurements of your actual roof, so it's a reliable ballpark for planning. Things like decking damage, extra layers or ventilation changes can only be confirmed in person. That's what the free inspection is for.

Will someone call me right away?

Only if you want us to. Your estimate comes to our team, and we'll follow up to answer questions. You're never obligated to schedule anything.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. If your roof has hail or wind damage, we can inspect it, document the damage and help you through the claim process.

What areas do you serve?

Carrollton and all of West Georgia, plus the greater Atlanta metro area.
